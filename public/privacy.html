Barely - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Barely is a small, offline-first daily tracker. It has no accounts and no analytics, and it does not sell, share, or transmit your data to us. This page explains what happens to your data, including the optional Google Drive sync.

What data Barely stores

Your tasks and settings are stored on your own device (in the browser's IndexedDB and localStorage). We do not operate a server that receives or stores your task data.

Export / Import backup

You can export a JSON file of your data and import it later. That file is created and read entirely on your device; it is only shared if you choose to move it somewhere yourself.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose to enable Google Drive sync, Barely requests permission to store a single file in your Google account's hidden, per-app storage area (the drive.appdata folder) using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope, and reads your email address (userinfo.email) only to show which account is connected.

  • The data is stored in your own Google Drive, not on our servers.
  • Only Barely, signed in as you, can access that file. Other apps cannot see it.
  • We do not receive, read, or retain your Google data. The browser talks to Google directly.
  • You can disconnect at any time in the app, and revoke access in your Google Account.

Barely's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder and grant notification permission, notifications are scheduled and shown by your own device/browser. No notification data leaves your device.
